Standard Charter Agreement & Regulations

Terms of Carriage & Charter Regulations

Fair, transparent, and legally binding rental guidelines governing all TransVoyage coach and traveller charter journeys across national highway networks.

1 Tariff Computation & Distance Rules

  • Outstation Minimum Distance: A minimum of 300 kilometres per calendar day (midnight to midnight) is applicable for all outstation bookings. If actual distance travelled is less than the minimum, billing is calculated on the minimum 300 km/day standard.
  • Garage-to-Garage Calculation: Kilometres and duty hours are computed starting from vehicle departure from our regional depot to its safe return to the depot.
  • Local City Packages: Standard local rentals are offered on an 8 Hours / 80 Kilometres basis. Additional hours and kilometres beyond the package are billed according to the agreed hourly and per-km surplus schedule.
  • Fuel & Maintenance: All base per-km tariffs include fuel costs, routine fluids, and vehicle preventive maintenance.

2 Driver Allowances & Working Hours

  • Driver Day Batta: A standard driver day allowance (₹400 for Tempo Travellers, ₹500 for Mini Buses, ₹600 for Volvo Coaches) is payable per calendar day to cover chauffeur food and meal expenses.
  • Night Allowance: Driving between 10:00 PM and 06:00 AM incurs an additional night allowance of ₹300 to ₹500 per night.
  • Driver Fatigue & Road Safety: In compliance with National Road Safety guidelines, maximum driving hours are capped at 10 hours within any 24-hour window. On trips exceeding 500 km per day or continuous overnight operations, a dual-chauffeur crew will be assigned.

3 Tolls, Parking & Interstate Permits

  • State Border Taxes & All-India Tourist Permits: All vehicles hold All-India Tourist Permits (AITP). Any state entry tax levied at interstate borders is payable at actuals against official computer-generated receipts, unless explicitly booked under an All-Inclusive Corporate SLA.
  • Toll Plazas & Parking Fees: Highway Fastag tolls and airport/monument parking charges are to be reimbursed by the passenger/corporate client at actual costs.
