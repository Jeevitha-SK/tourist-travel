Planning a group road trip with 10 to 18 family members? Instead of coordinating three separate cars where conversations are split and drivers are stressed, chartering a luxury Tempo Traveller has become India’s most favored vacation choice.

1. Choosing the Right Seating Configuration

Tempo Travellers are not one-size-fits-all. In 2026, the market offers three distinct cabin formats:

  • 9 & 12-Seater Luxury (1x1 Layout): Ideal for 8–10 passengers who desire individual captain recliner chairs and maximum legroom. Excellent for elderly family members who need ample stretch space.
  • 16 & 17-Seater Force Urbania (2x1 Layout): Next-generation European monocoque van. Features independent suspension, substantially lower cabin noise, and individual roof AC vents for each passenger.
  • 20-Seater Maharaja Luxury (2x2 Layout): Perfect for larger family groups and wedding party shuttles. High luggage boot volume with starlight ambient interior ceiling lights.

Pro Travel Tip: Always Reserve 2 Extra Seats for Luggage

If you have 12 passengers traveling for 5 days with large trolley suitcases, book a 16-seater or 17-seater Urbania rather than a tight 12-seater. The extra rear row ensures zero bags spill into passenger leg areas.

2. How Per-Kilometer Billing Actually Works

Many first-time travelers get confused by rental quotes. Here is the transparent breakdown guaranteed by TransVoyage:

  1. Minimum Daily KM: For outstation trips, vehicles have a standard minimum threshold of 250 km per calendar day. Even if you drive 180 km, the minimum 250 km is charged. If you drive 340 km, you pay for the actual 340 km.
  2. Driver Batta: A flat daily allowance (₹500 to ₹700 per day) that covers the driver’s food and stay.
  3. Tolls & State Permits: Paid at actuals via electronic FASTag or prepaid as a lump-sum package.

3. Essential Safety Features to Confirm Before Boarding

Never compromise on passenger safety on mountainous or night highways. Always verify:

  • Commercial All India Tourist Permit (AITP) and valid fitness certificate.
  • Speed governor physically capped at 80 km/h in accordance with RTO directives.
  • Active GPS tracking enabled so family members can monitor live route speed.
  • Spare tire, jack, and emergency medical kit onboard.

At TransVoyage, every vehicle is subjected to an 18-point mechanical audit before each dispatch, including brake pad testing, AC filter sanitization, and tire tread depth checks.
