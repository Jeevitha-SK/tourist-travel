Privacy Policy & Data Security Standards

Effective January 2026. How TransVoyage handles your reservation details, live GPS tracking coordinates, and personal payment credentials with bank-grade encryption.

256-Bit SSL Encrypted Transmission
Zero Data Sales Never Shared or Sold

1 Information We Collect

When you make an enquiry, request a tariff quotation, or confirm a tourist vehicle reservation with TransVoyage, we collect the necessary personal and travel itinerary information to provide commercial carriage:

  • Contact Information: Full name, verified mobile/WhatsApp number, and email address.
  • Journey Coordinates: Pickup depot/address, intermediate halt locations, outstation destination cities, and calendar travel dates.
  • Group Information: Passenger head count, senior citizen assistance notes, and luggage volume specifications.
  • Corporate Billing Data: Company legal entity name, GSTIN number, and registered office address for GST input tax credit invoices.

2 Vehicle Telematics & GPS Tracking

Every vehicle in the TransVoyage fleet (Tempo Travellers, Mini Buses, Volvo Coaches) is equipped with AIS-140 compliant GPS telematics and speed governors mandated by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH):

  • Live Location Tracking: GPS coordinates are transmitted every 10 seconds to our centralized operations radar to monitor schedule adherence and highway safety.
  • Safety Governors: Speed logs are monitored to enforce the maximum statutory 80 km/h commercial tourist bus limit.
  • SOS Switch Log: In the event of an onboard emergency, passenger panic switches immediately alert our 24/7 central dispatch and nearest state highway patrol.
  • Privacy Guarantee: Passenger conversations inside the cabin are never recorded. Only vehicular telemetry, speed, and location coordinates are logged.

3 How We Protect and Store Your Data

We treat your travel privacy with utmost confidentiality:

  • No Third-Party Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade your contact details or travel destinations to marketing brokers.
  • Local Storage Resilience: When interacting with our instant fare calculator and customer dashboard, session preferences and demo trip records are stored in your browser's local cache (tv_custom_bookings), allowing offline persistence without unneeded server profiling.
  • Payment Processing: Online advance payments and card details are processed via PCI-DSS compliant banking gateways using 256-bit SSL encryption. TransVoyage never stores raw credit/debit card numbers or CVVs on its servers.
