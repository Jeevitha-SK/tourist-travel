What is Included in Your Tariff

  • Fuel costs (Diesel) for the entire agreed itinerary.
  • Vehicle charter with certified commercial tourist chauffeur.
  • Clean, sanitized vehicle with functional AC throughout journey.
  • 24/7 central live GPS fleet tracking and roadside breakdown backup.
  • First aid medical kit and fire extinguishers onboard.

State Permits, Tolls & Policies

  • Highway FASTag tolls and interstate border entry taxes at actuals.
  • Parking and monument entry charges paid directly by customer.
  • Calendar day calculation: 00:00 midnight to 23:59 represents 1 day.
  • Night driving allowance (₹300 - ₹800 based on capacity) between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM.
  • 5% GST applicable on all passenger road transport invoices.
