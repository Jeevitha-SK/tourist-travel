Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about tariffs, driver allowances, toll taxes, and highway roadside guarantees.

Outstation fares are calculated based on the agreed per-kilometre tariff rate with an industry-standard minimum run of 300 kilometres per calendar day (midnight to midnight). If you travel 700 km over 2 days, billing is 700 km × rate. If you travel only 450 km over 2 days, billing is for the minimum 600 km (2 days × 300 km). Fuel and routine vehicle maintenance are fully included in the base rate.
Driver day allowance (batta) covers food and living costs for our professional chauffeurs, ranging from ₹400/day for Tempo Travellers to ₹600/day for Volvo Coaches. An additional night allowance of ₹300 to ₹500 is only applicable if the vehicle operates between 10:00 PM and 06:00 AM.
In standard rental quotes, highway Fastag tolls, state border permit taxes, and monument/airport parking are payable at actuals against government computer-generated receipts. For corporate and institutional accounts, we also offer All-Inclusive SLAs where all tolls and interstate taxes are pre-estimated and bundled into one consolidated GST tax invoice.
TransVoyage maintains 25+ regional transit depots with standby replacement coaches. In the rare event of a tire puncture or mechanical problem, our 24/7 central dispatch triggers immediate on-site mechanic dispatch or mobilizes an equivalent replacement vehicle from the nearest transit hub with a guaranteed 60-minute roadside response.
